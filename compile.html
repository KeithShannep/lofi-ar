LoFi Layers AR — Target Compiler

Upload the artwork image(s) you want the AR app to recognize. This compiles a real targets.mind file entirely in your browser (nothing is uploaded anywhere) — download it and drop it into assets/targets.mind, replacing the placeholder.

Tips for good tracking: use high-contrast, detail-rich artwork (avoid large flat/blank areas). Flat vector line-art with little texture tracks poorly — a busier illustration works better. Compile time scales with image size; large images may take a bit.
